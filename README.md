# Computational Modeling - SFedU

A research portfolio documenting my MSc journey in Computational Modeling in Technology and Finance at Southern Federal University (SFedU), Rostov-on-Don, Russia.

This repository works as an ongoing research space where I explore the connections between:

1. Computational Modeling
2. Machine Learning
3. Data Analysis
4. Mathematical Modeling
5. Financial Modeling
6. Artificial Intelligence
7. Technology and Finance

## Research Vision

My goal is to build rigorous computational and mathematical frameworks that address real-world problems in technology and finance, with a particular focus on emerging economies and African markets.
